Practitioner Manas Saengkaew and Practitioner Araya Thatburom serve as judges at the DPU MASTERPIECE Thai massage competition

29 November 2026 | DPU MASTERPIECE : Where Excellence Becomes Legendary

"An honour and a source of pride in Thai traditional medicine 🇹🇭✨

It is a great honour to serve as one of the judges of the Thai massage competition (Thai Massage Judge) at the standard-setting event DPU MASTERPIECE : Where Excellence Becomes Legendary in collaboration with the College of Integrative Medicine, Dhurakij Pundit University (CIMw DPU), WKI and DARAN.

Serving as a judge on 29 November 2026 is not only about assessing skill and ability, but about helping to select and raise the professional standards of Thai massage to the international level with value and grace.
